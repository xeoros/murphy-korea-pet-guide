Where to go and stay with Murphy

Pick a regional route and check the pet rules before visiting. These flexible outings can fit around your travel dates.

Seoul

Must-go: Seoul Forest
Optional: Gyeongui Line Forest Park

Do Seoul Forest in the morning. Save the Hongdae walk for a separate afternoon or a check-in day if you stay in Mapo.

Busan

Must-go: Gwangalli Seafront Walk
Optional: Haeundae Pet Playground

Walk the Gwangalli seafront in daylight or before sunset. Add the playground if you are already heading east and admission is confirmed.

Gyeonggi-do

Must-go: Ilsan Lake Park
Optional: Thinking Dog

Walk the lake in the morning and add the café if time allows. Keep a Gapyeong overnight separate from this Goyang outing.

Evidence and access guide

Friendly: reported dedicated pet services or facilities. Allowed: reported admission without dedicated services. Restricted: reported prohibition. These are editorial classifications, not official certifications.

Reported: prior guide claims access. Conditional: restrictions or partial access. Historical: older information that may no longer apply. No reported: prior guide reports prohibition. Unknown: insufficient information. For stays, indoor access refers to the booked pet room or rental. Check shared spaces separately.

Check the Food Safety Korea register for food establishments and use Naver Map venue announcements to confirm current policies. Older source leads vary in strength. Lodging policies and recommendation sources have their own checked dates.
